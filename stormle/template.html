The two modes

Calm — an answer is fixed before you start, from the day's date. Ordinary Wordle, with the barometer running.

Storm — the host never commits to a word. Each time you guess it replies with whichever pattern keeps the most answers alive, so the field shrinks as slowly as the rules allow. It never lies: every reply it gives is consistent with at least one real word, and it must still be beaten in six. Here the barometer stops being decoration — it is the only way to see how badly you are being stalled.
