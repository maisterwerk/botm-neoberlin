# Domain 2 — a deliberately unrelated research question

Domain 1 was adversarial mechanism design (incentives, sybil resistance). Domain 2 was chosen to
share none of that structure: it is a measurement / causal-inference problem.

> A language model scores 12 points higher on a public benchmark after a new training run. Design a
> protocol that determines whether that gain is genuine capability or benchmark contamination (test
> data leaked into training). Specify it precisely enough to execute, name what could confound it,
> define at least one measurable statistic with a decision threshold, and say how you would falsify
> your own protocol.

Same lab topology, same role prompts (re-worded for the domain), same effort-matched solo control,
same blind judging — but this time every judge gets the IDENTICAL neutral framing, fixing the
model/framing confound a reviewer found in domain 1.
